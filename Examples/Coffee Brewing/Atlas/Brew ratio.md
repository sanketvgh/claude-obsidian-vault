# Brew ratio

> The measured ratio of coffee to water — the actual control for strength and extraction, instead of eyeballing it.

## Elaboration
- Typical espresso ratio: 1:2 (18g coffee → 36g output)
- Higher ratio (more water) → higher extraction, lighter body
- Should be weighed, not judged by cup fill level or time alone

## Connections
- Defined by [[Dose]] and output weight together
- Works alongside [[Grind size]] to hit a target [[Extraction]]
