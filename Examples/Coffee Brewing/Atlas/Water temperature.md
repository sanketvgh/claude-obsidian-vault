# Water temperature

> Water that's too cool under-extracts; too hot over-extracts and can scald the coffee — most espresso machines target roughly 90-96°C.

## Elaboration
- Less impactful than grind size or ratio, but still a real lever
- Easy to overlook because most machines fix it for you

## Connections
- A secondary control on [[Extraction]], behind [[Grind size]] and [[Brew ratio]]
