# Extraction

> Extraction is how much soluble material moves from the coffee grounds into the water — measurable as a percentage, not just a taste.

## Elaboration
- Target range is roughly 18-22% extraction yield
- Under-extracted (too low) tastes sour/sharp
- Over-extracted (too high) tastes bitter/hollow
- It's the actual variable being controlled — grind size, ratio, and temperature are just levers that move it

## Connections
- Controlled primarily by [[Grind size]] — finer grind slows water flow, increasing extraction
- Also shaped by [[Brew ratio]] and [[Dose]] — more water relative to coffee pulls more out
- [[Water temperature]] is a secondary lever on the same target
- Symptom-based troubleshooting ("sour → grind finer") is really just extraction-adjustment in disguise
