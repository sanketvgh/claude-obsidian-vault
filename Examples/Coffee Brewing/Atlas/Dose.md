# Dose

> The weight of dry coffee grounds used for a shot — the other half of [[Brew ratio]], and the number everything else gets measured against.

## Elaboration
- Typically 18-20g for a double espresso basket
- Changing dose without adjusting ratio silently changes strength

## Connections
- Paired with output weight to define [[Brew ratio]]
- A fixed reference point for comparing [[Grind size]] changes day to day
