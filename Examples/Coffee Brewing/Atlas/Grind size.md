# Grind size

> The main lever for controlling extraction speed — finer grind slows water flow and increases extraction; coarser speeds it up and decreases it.

## Elaboration
- Espresso needs a fine, consistent grind because contact time is short (~25-30s)
- Too fine → water can't pass through, shot chokes, over-extracts
- Too coarse → water rushes through, under-extracts, tastes sour

## Connections
- Directly controls [[Extraction]]
- Interacts with [[Brew ratio]] — changing one often means re-adjusting the other
