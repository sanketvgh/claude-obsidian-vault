# How to Make the Best Coffee at Home

## Summary (in my own words)
Espresso quality comes down to a few controllable variables: grind size, dose, brew ratio, and water temperature. Extraction is a measurable target (roughly 18-22% extraction yield), not just a taste preference — sour means under-extracted, bitter means over-extracted.

## Key points
- Grind size is the main lever for extraction speed
- Brew ratio (coffee:water) should be measured, not eyeballed
- Consistency matters more than any single "perfect" setting
